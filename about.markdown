# About Me

## Who I Am

Hi, I'm **Gino Paul Mendoza Capio**, a software engineer with nearly a decade of experience building scalable web applications and fostering inclusive tech communities. I'm passionate about clean code, mentoring developers, and the "Each One, Teach One" philosophy.

## What I Do

Currently, I'm a **Software Developer at Pixite Inc** (March 2021 - Present), where I work remotely building innovative solutions using:
- Ruby on Rails
- Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
- Elasticsearch
- Redis
- Amazon S3

## My Journey

### From Healthcare to Tech

My journey into software engineering is a bit unique. I started in healthcare at Swedish Covenant Hospital in Chicago, where I taught myself how to communicate "I need to draw blood" in 7 different languages (Russian, Vietnamese, Spanish, Greek, Korean, Hindi, and Arabic) to better serve patients. This experience taught me the value of clear communication and meeting people where they are—principles I carry into my work as a developer.

### Breaking Into Tech

In 2015, I took the leap and enrolled in **Dev Bootcamp**, an immersive web development program. For my final project, my team and I learned React Native and Firebase from scratch and built a complete mobile app called "Snap Drop" in just 7 days.

### Professional Growth

Since then, I've worked across various roles and companies:

- **Great Hearts Initiatives** (2016-2020): Led development of Great Hearts Community, the world's largest online giving circle with 4000+ users who generated over 150,000 actions for good. Built and maintained features using Ruby on Rails, React, Spring, and GraphQL.

- **Affect X Technologies** (2019-2020): Built reactive interfaces with live data visualization and established Bluetooth communications between hardware and iOS apps with Google Cloud Platform integration.

- **On Point Technology** (2020-2021): Software engineering with Java and Spring Framework.

### Community Building

In August 2016, I founded **CoolCatsCoding**, a growing collective of learners consisting of current software engineers and people interested in breaking into tech. We meet regularly to foster a "Each One, Teach One" environment.

One of our notable collaborations was **Giving Profiles**, where CoolCatsCoding members worked with researchers from MIT and Harvard to build an open-source social network based on donations and philanthropy.

## Beyond Code

Before my tech career, I:
- Founded **Loyola BREAKERS**, a cultural organization educating others about Hip-Hop culture and social justice
- Automated billing calculations at Swedish Covenant Hospital using Excel, reducing human errors
- Designed logos, brochures, and business cards as an Administrative Coordinator
- Worked as a Brand Ambassador engaging consumers in promotional activities

## Speaking & Advocacy

I'm committed to making tech more inclusive and accessible. I've spoken at various venues about diversity, career transitions, Hip Hop culture, and education:

- **University of Virginia** (2022) - Guest speaker on Asian Americans & Popular Culture, discussing life as a bboy (breakdancer), Asian immigrant journey, and path into tech
- **Northwestern University** (2019) - Asian-American Studies Speaker on Hip Hop's cultural impact
- **Difference Engine** (2017) - Fireside chat on non-traditional paths into tech
- **Noble Charter School** (2017) - STEM Expo presentation for high school students  
- **UIC #IAMFIRST** (2017) - Tech diversity panel on experiences as an immigrant and person of color in tech

## Philosophy

I believe in:
- **Each One, Teach One**: Sharing knowledge and lifting others up
- **Diversity & Inclusion**: Creating spaces where everyone can learn and grow
- **Clean, maintainable code**: Building for the long term
- **Giving back**: Speaking, mentoring, and supporting the next generation
- **Continuous learning**: Technology evolves, and so should we
