# Welcome to My Blog

Hey there! 👋

Welcome to my tech blog. I'm Gino Paul Mendoza Capio, a software developer with nearly a decade of experience building web and mobile applications. This is where I'll be sharing my journey, insights, and lessons learned from the world of software engineering.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about topics including:

- **Ruby on Rails** - My primary framework for the past 8+ years
- **iOS Development with Swift** - Native mobile app development
- **React Native** - Cross-platform mobile development
- **Software Architecture** - Design patterns and best practices
- **Career Development** - Lessons from my journey from healthcare to tech
- **Mentorship & Community** - Building inclusive tech communities through "Each One, Teach One"
- **Open Source** - Contributions and collaborations

## Why I'm Starting This Blog

As the founder of [CoolCatsCoding](https://www.instagram.com/coolcatscoding/), I've always believed in the "Each One, Teach One" philosophy. This blog is an extension of that belief - a way to share knowledge, document my learning, and hopefully help others on their journey.

## A Bit About Me

I've had an interesting path into tech. Before becoming a software engineer, I worked in healthcare at Swedish Covenant Hospital, where I taught myself how to say "I need to draw blood" in 7 different languages (Russian, Vietnamese, Spanish, Greek, Korean, Hindi, and Arabic) to better serve diverse patients. That experience taught me the power of communication and meeting people where they are - principles I carry into software development.

Since then, I've:
- Built the **world's largest online giving circle** (Great Hearts Community) with 4,000+ users generating 150,000+ actions for good
- Collaborated with **MIT and Harvard researchers** on an open-source social network
- Founded and led **CoolCatsCoding** for 9+ years
- Worked across web and mobile platforms with Ruby on Rails, Swift, React Native, and more
